# Product Decisions

Decision 001

Top3 exists to help people discover one another through shared taste.

Reason

Defines the product.

---

Decision 002

Use "Following" instead of "Followed."

Reason

Represents an ongoing relationship.

---

Decision 003

Recommendations require at least one shared ranked item.

Reason

Every recommendation should feel meaningful.

---

Decision 004

Private profiles never appear in recommendations.

Reason

Respect privacy settings.

---

Decision 005

Shared picks use lavender highlights.

Reason

Reinforces connection instead of competition.

---

Decision 006

FollowButton is a reusable component.

Reason

Consistency.
