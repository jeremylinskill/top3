# Top3 Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 26, 2026

Top3 is a social discovery app that lets people create and share ranked Top 3 lists, discover people with similar tastes, and interact with other members of the Top3 community.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Top3 collects and processes, how that information is used and shared, the choices available to you, and how you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information.

Top3 is operated by **Jeremy Linskill**, based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

## 1. Information We Collect and Process

The information Top3 collects or processes depends on how you use the service.

### Account and Authentication Information

When you create or use a Top3 account, we process information necessary to create, authenticate, and maintain your account.

This may include:

- your email address;
- authentication information necessary to create and maintain your account; and
- information provided through an authentication provider if you choose to sign in using Apple or Google.

Email and password authentication is handled using Supabase Auth. Top3 does not itself store your password in its application database.

If you use **Sign in with Apple**, Apple may provide Top3 with information such as your name and email address, depending on the information you choose to share and Apple's privacy settings. Top3 uses authentication information provided by Apple to authenticate your account through Supabase.

If you use **Google Sign-In**, Google provides authentication information necessary to authenticate your Top3 account through Supabase.

Top3 does not receive your Apple ID password, Google password, or the password for another third-party identity provider.

### Profile Information

You may provide information for your Top3 profile, including:

- username;
- display name;
- optional biography;
- optional profile image or avatar; and
- account visibility setting.

Some profile information is intended to be visible to other people as part of Top3's social experience, subject to the privacy and access controls described below.

### Profile Images and Photo-Library Access

If you choose to add or change a profile image, Top3 requests permission to access your device's photo library so that you can select an image.

Top3 accesses the photo library when you choose to use a feature that requires selecting an image. The selected profile image is uploaded and stored using Top3's cloud infrastructure.

Top3 does not currently request camera permission.

### Lists and Other Content You Create

Top3 stores information you create or publish through the service, including:

- Top 3 lists;
- categories and topics associated with lists;
- ranked items within your lists;
- comments; and
- other content you intentionally submit through Top3.

Published lists are designed to be shared and discovered by other people, subject to your account visibility and Top3's access controls.

### Social Activity

Top3 stores information about interactions within the service, including:

- accounts you follow;
- accounts that follow you;
- follow requests;
- likes;
- comments;
- blocking relationships; and
- interactions that generate in-app notifications.

We use this information to provide Top3's social features and to determine what content and people you can see or interact with.

### Reports and Moderation Information

If you report a user, list, comment, or other supported content, Top3 may collect:

- your Top3 user identifier;
- the user associated with the reported content;
- the type of content being reported;
- the identifier of the reported content;
- the reason for the report; and
- any additional details you choose to provide.

This information is used to review reports, enforce Top3's rules, protect users, investigate misuse, and maintain the safety and integrity of the service.

Information associated with reports and moderation actions may be retained where reasonably necessary for safety, moderation, recordkeeping, dispute resolution, prevention of abuse, or legal compliance.

### Search Information

When you search for entertainment content, information necessary to perform the search is sent to the relevant service used to retrieve results.

Depending on the type of content, Top3 currently uses services including:

- The Movie Database (TMDb) for movies and television;
- Google Books for books;
- Open Library as a book-search source or fallback;
- Apple Music for songs, albums, and artists; and
- IGDB/Twitch for video games.

Some provider requests are made through Top3's backend infrastructure rather than directly from your device.

Search terms or other information necessary to retrieve results may therefore be transmitted to the relevant content provider or processed through Top3's backend services.

Top3 also maintains a limited list of up to 10 recent searches on your device to make it easier to return to previous searches. This recent-search history is stored locally on the device and associated locally with your Top3 account rather than stored as part of your server-side Top3 profile.

### Media Previews and Trailers

Top3 may allow you to play media associated with items displayed in the app.

For example:

- music previews may be provided using Apple Music media; and
- movie and television trailers may be discovered through TMDb and played using YouTube.

When you choose to play external media, your device may communicate with the relevant media provider to retrieve or play that content.

Those providers may process technical information associated with the request according to their own privacy practices.

### Usage and Analytics Information

Top3 uses product analytics to understand how people use the app and to improve the service.

Analytics may include information about interactions such as:

- creating an account;
- completing onboarding;
- starting, completing, publishing, or editing a list;
- performing a search;
- adding an item to a list;
- viewing Discover;
- viewing a profile;
- following another user;
- liking a collection;
- adding a comment;
- viewing Taste Match;
- viewing or sharing a collection; and
- opening a notification.

Analytics events may include limited contextual information such as the relevant category, source, ranking position, or number of ranked items.

When you are signed in, analytics activity may be associated with your Top3 user identifier so that we can understand how the service is being used.

Top3 currently uses **Amplitude** to provide product analytics.

Amplitude may process a pseudonymous device identifier generated for analytics purposes. When you are signed in, Top3 also associates analytics activity with your Top3 user identifier.

Top3 configures the Amplitude SDK not to collect advertising ID, IP address, carrier, device manufacturer, device model, language, operating-system name or version, or platform information through Amplitude's tracking options.

Top3 uses Amplitude for product analytics rather than third-party advertising.

### Device and Local Application Information

Top3 stores certain information locally on your device that is necessary to operate the app, maintain your authentication session, remember application state, and provide features such as recent searches.

This may include authentication/session information, locally cached application data, and application preferences.

## 4. Public and Private Information

Top3 is a social service. Some information you choose to provide or publish is therefore intended to be seen by other people.

Depending on your account settings and how you use Top3, this may include:

- username;
- display name;
- profile image;
- biography;
- published Top 3 lists;
- comments; and
- social information surfaced by Top3.

If your account is **public**, your profile information and published lists may be visible to other Top3 users.

Published public lists may also be accessible to people who receive a shared link without requiring them to sign in, where Top3 supports signed-out viewing.

If your account is **private**, access to your published lists is restricted according to Top3's private-account and approved-follower rules.

Your profile may still appear in user search so that other users can find you and request to follow you.

Blocking another user further restricts visibility and interaction between the accounts according to Top3's blocking features.

You should avoid including sensitive personal information in your username, biography, comments, lists, or other content you choose to make visible through Top3.

Top3 generally retains account-associated information while your account remains active and as needed to provide the features of the service.

When you delete content or your account, Top3 removes or requests deletion of information as described in this Privacy Policy and according to the application's implemented deletion processes.

Some information may be retained where reasonably necessary for:

- moderation and safety records;
- security and prevention of abuse;
- investigation of misuse;

- compliance with legal obligations; or
- other legitimate operational purposes permitted by applicable law.

Reported or moderated content may therefore be retained in limited moderation records even when it is no longer publicly visible through Top3.

Third-party processors, backup systems, or other technical systems may require additional time to complete deletion after a deletion request is initiated.

Top3 does not intentionally retain personal information longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, subject to legitimate legal, safety, security, moderation, and operational requirements.

## 8. Account and Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your Top3 account using the account-deletion controls available within the app.

The deletion flow communicates that account deletion is permanent.

When account deletion succeeds, Top3's implemented deletion process is designed to remove the authentication account and associated Top3 account data according to Top3's database and account relationships.

This includes account-associated information such as:

- your profile;
- published lists;
- comments;
- likes;
- follows and follow relationships; and
- other account-associated records handled by the deletion process.

Where applicable, Top3 also:

- removes your stored profile image;
- submits a request to Amplitude to delete analytics information associated with your Top3 user identifier; and
- revokes associated Sign in with Apple authorization for Apple-authenticated accounts.

When account deletion succeeds, Top3 also clears the recent-search history stored locally for that Top3 account on the device used to perform the deletion.

Some information may be retained where reasonably necessary for moderation records, safety and security, prevention of abuse, investigation of misuse, dispute resolution, legal obligations, or other legitimate purposes permitted by applicable law.

Third-party deletion requests may require additional time to complete.

Other cached or operating-system-managed information may remain temporarily until cleared by the application or operating system, or until the app is removed from the device.

You may contact **jeremylinskill@gmail.com** if you have questions about deleting your account or personal information.

## 12. Children and Teen Users

Top3 is intended for users who are **13 years of age or older**.

Children under 13 are not permitted to create a Top3 account.

Top3 is not designed or directed specifically to children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

Top3 does not currently collect a user's birthdate or perform age verification as part of account creation. Users are responsible for complying with Top3's minimum-age requirement.

If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account or provided personal information through Top3, we may take steps to remove the account and associated information as appropriate.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information to Top3, please contact **jeremylinskill@gmail.com**.

We recognize that teenagers may require additional care when making privacy decisions and aim to provide understandable information and privacy controls that allow users to make informed choices about their participation in Top3.
